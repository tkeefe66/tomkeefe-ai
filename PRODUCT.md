# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary: the GTM/RevOps professional community — peers who evaluate Tom's thinking and craft by how he talks about GTM systems. Secondary: hiring managers and recruiters scanning for signal on a candidate/leader. Tertiary: personal record for Tom himself. They arrive via a shared link or search, skim in under a minute, and decide whether this person is worth following/hiring based on tone and evidence, not a long read.

## Product Purpose

A single-page personal portfolio for Tom Keefe (Director of GTM Experts, Demandbase) that puts principles and ideas front and center, backed by real project evidence — modeled loosely on chadholdorf.com. It exists to position Tom as a GTM Engineer/Ops builder who ships, not just theorizes. Success: a visitor forms a strong, accurate impression of Tom's stance and body of work in under two minutes, on any device.

## Brand Personality

Confident, direct, a little edge. Declarative sentences, numbers-forward. Not corporate-polished, not startup-cute — closer to a builder's manifesto than a resume. Visual system: bold and high-contrast (near-black background, off-white type, exactly one electric accent color), oversized display numerals, tight grid, generous vertical rhythm.

## Anti-references

Must not look like a default Tailwind/shadcn template, a generic SaaS marketing site, or a stock "AI-generated portfolio" (no cream/sand neutral body background, no gradient text, no tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section, no identical icon+heading+text card grids, no side-stripe accent borders). Should not read as safe or interchangeable — the whole point is a distinct point of view, expressed visually as well as verbally.

## Design Principles

- Confidence over decoration: state things plainly, at scale, and let typography carry the weight instead of ornament.
- Evidence over claims: every principle and stat is paired with a visible honesty marker (draft tags, placeholder styling) rather than faked polish.
- One accent, used deliberately: a single electric color earns attention because it is scarce.
- Systems thinking made visible: tight grid, numbered sequences where order is real information (principles list), generous rhythm between sections so the page breathes.
- Ship what's true today: unfinished content (placeholder stats, draft principles, coming-soon links) is shown as intentionally unfinished, never hidden or faked.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast against the near-black background, large/display text ≥3:1. Full keyboard navigation for nav anchors and contact links. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for any scroll/entrance animation. No content conveyed by color alone (status badges pair color with text label).
